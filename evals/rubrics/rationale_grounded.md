Score whether each outfit's written explanation (`rationale` and
`weather_fit`) is true of the items actually in that outfit and of the
forecast in the input.

1.0 — Every claim can be checked against the listed items (colors, fabric,
warmth, formality, tags) or the forecast, and holds.
0.7 — Accurate overall, with one loose or generic claim that is not wrong.
0.4 — At least one explanation states something false about an item or the
forecast (a fabric or color the item doesn't have, protection it isn't tagged
for, weather that isn't in the forecast).
0.0 — The explanations are mostly unrelated to the items shown.

Warmth runs 0 (hot weather only) to 5 (freezing). Formality runs 1 (loungewear)
to 5 (formal).

Judge only whether the explanations are accurate. Whether the outfits are a
good choice for the occasion is scored by another check.
