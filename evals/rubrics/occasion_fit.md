Score how well the suggested outfits suit the occasion and the forecast given
in the input.

1.0 — Every outfit is something a person would sensibly wear to this occasion
in this weather.
0.7 — The outfits work, but one is a noticeably weaker fit for the occasion or
the conditions than the others.
0.4 — At least one outfit is clearly wrong for the occasion or the weather
(too formal, too casual, too warm, too light, no protection from stated rain
or wind when the closet items shown could have provided it).
0.0 — None of the outfits suit the occasion or the weather.

If the input gives no occasion, judge against the forecast alone.

Judge only suitability. Whether the written rationale is accurate, and whether
an outfit is structurally complete, are scored by other checks.
